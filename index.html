RACE REGULATIONS

All constructors must strictly adhere to the following hardware telemetry and fabrication protocols.

  • From-Scratch Fabrication: No pre-assembled robotic chassis or pre-coded microcontrollers are allowed. All soldering and assembly must occur within the 24-hour race window.
  • Power Limits: High-voltage components exceeding 24V DC or 110V AC require explicit safety sign-off from the Pit Lane Mentors prior to testing.
  • Component Scrutineering: All raw components (sensors, Arduino/STM boards, bare PCBs) will be inspected at the start line to ensure no pre-built advantage.
  • Pit Box Safety: Soldering irons and heat guns must only be used in designated fire-safe Pit Boxes. Proper eye protection is mandatory during fabrication.
  • Open-Source Telemetry: Any software used to visualize the hardware data must be pushed to a public repository for the judges to verify before the checkered flag.
